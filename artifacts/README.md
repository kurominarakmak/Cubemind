# Experiment artifacts

V2.1's `expert-cross-policy.pt` and `expert-cross-training.json` are documented in [`docs/model_card_expert_cross.md`](../docs/model_card_expert_cross.md). `algorithm-preference-policy.pt` and `algorithm-preference-report.json` contain the random-initialized OLL/PLL ranker and its solver-disjoint human-choice audit; only 2,169 candidate-specific replay-verified choices were retained. `neurosymbolic-100-benchmark.json` is the paired fresh-scramble neural-generation versus algorithm-memory evaluation, and `neurosymbolic-preference-100-benchmark.json` adds the learned ranking on the same scrambles. An incomplete JSON is not a benchmark result.

The original `mlp-*` and `transformer-*` checkpoints were trained from random initialization on synthetic shortest-Cross data. `reco-cfop-context.pt` is a from-scratch next-action model trained on local reco.nz human reconstruction rows; its raw/processed corpus is ignored by Git. `zbll-classifier.pt` is trained from random initialization on simulator-generated algorithm-case poses, not human solves.

The adjacent JSONL files contain Cross training curves and `eval-*.json` contains held-out synthetic Cross results. `reco-cfop-eval.json`, `zbll-classifier-eval.json`, and `cfop-policy-benchmark.json` contain the extension metrics. `cfop-policy-benchmark.json` reports solver-compute timing, not human execution or recognition time.

`neural-cfop-policy.pt` is a from-scratch phase-aware PyTorch policy/value model trained on simulator-verified synthetic CFOP teacher paths, DAgger-style teacher relabeling after the project Cross MLP, and synthetic ZBLL algorithm paths. Its training report is `neural-cfop-training.json`; the latest held-out report is `neural-cfop-benchmark-hybrid-search.json`. On that 100-scramble run, synthetic and reco.nz-F2L hybrid search each completed Cross on 99/100, F2L on 0/100, and full solves on 0/100; the deterministic comparison solved 100/100. `neural-cfop-benchmark-dagger.json` and `neural-cfop-benchmark-human-hybrid.json` preserve earlier fresh-seed results; `neural-cfop-benchmark-final.json` and `neural-cfop-benchmark-pre-dagger.json` preserve the first pre-DAgger run, while `neural-cfop-benchmark-initial.json` is the Cross-bottleneck diagnostic. The phase/value model uses no reco.nz action rows; the separately reported hybrid uses the reco.nz F2L checkpoint. No human execution or recognition time is inferred.

`hierarchical-f2l.pt` is a separate random-initialized PyTorch action/value/pair-selector trained on source-labeled reco.nz F2L rows, simulator-generated CFOP teacher and DAgger paths, and 24 verified on-policy recovery trajectories. Its training metrics are in `hierarchical-f2l-training.json`. `hierarchical-f2l-benchmark.json` contains 100 held-out end-to-end Cross+F2L cases and independently replay-verified 98/100 hierarchical neural-search completions, compared with 22/100 for uniform-action search and 0/100 for greedy and flat F2L modes. The full simulator reference solved 100/100. `hierarchical-f2l-development-diagnostics.json` preserves the smaller value-head, Cross-source, and held-out curriculum-stage checks. The benchmark's exact Cross/pair pattern distances are search heuristics; they provide no F2L move sequence and are not a fallback. This milestone stops before the Last Layer.

`canonical-expert-f2l.pt` and `expert-aware-pair-selector.pt` are new random-initialized human-only models trained on solver-disjoint reco.nz rows. `expert-aware-f2l-evaluation.json` reports held-out next-action and pair-order agreement and validation-only calibration. Calibration selected the expert-only action endpoint. `expert-aware-f2l-benchmark.json` contains 100 fresh, replay-verified comparisons: the expert-aware route completed F2L on 95/100, while the legal-normalized planner completed 90/100. Its same-scramble exact-original planner run is merged into the report; raw rows are also in `expert-aware-original-planner-100.json`. The 20-case pilot is preserved at `expert-aware-f2l-benchmark-20-case-pilot.json`; component ablations are in `expert-aware-f2l-ablation-20-case.json`. The expert-aware route did not reduce move count.

`last-layer-policy.pt` is the selected random-initialized OLL/PLL/ZBLL policy/value MLP. `last-layer-policy-seed*.pt` preserve each training seed. `last-layer-training.json` records source-separated training, solver-held-out human action agreement, synthetic held-out policy accuracy, and the selected seed. Human and simulator-generated trajectories are separate files under ignored `data/processed/last_layer/`; its manifest records source hashes, solver splits, synthetic scramble splits, and ZBLL case-identity splits. `last-layer-stage-validation-wide-budget.json` is the validation-only search selection artifact. On the separate test split, `last-layer-benchmark.json` records 158/158 PLL, 158/158 OLL→PLL, and 194/200 eligible ZBLL stage completions plus a 100-scramble end-to-end comparison. Neural-guided EO-aware search solved 90/100; the neural OLL→PLL route solved 93/100, greedy solved 10/100, value-only search 0/100, and the deterministic full reference 100/100. It includes Wilson intervals, per-scramble phase moves and states, independent replay checks, action-recognition comparison, and failure causes. The classifier and algorithm table do not choose neural moves.

`demo-full-solve.jpg` captures the Streamlit full-solver view after it independently replay-verified a neural ZBLL solve from the final benchmark set.

The local-only `v2/` experiment contains random-initialized synthetic-only, reco.nz-human-only, and mixed ZBLS policy/value/EO checkpoints; `zbls-training-evaluation.json` and `zbls-manifest.json` record source-separated counts, case/solver splits, metrics, and hashes. `zbls-search-confirmatory.json` preserves the 36-case case-disjoint neural-guidance comparison and its unsuccessful rows. `full-solve-smoke.json` is a one-scramble white/yellow integration check, not a benchmark. `full-solve-benchmark.json` contains 100 paired fresh scrambles: conventional and EO-aware neural routes each solved 94/100; bounded candidate selection solved 95/100; the deterministic CFOP reference solved 100/100. It records checkpoint hashes, route candidates, paired tests, failures, move counts, search cost, and Wilson intervals. Human-source V2 weights remain local research artifacts; do not treat them as a public model release without confirming redistribution rights.
